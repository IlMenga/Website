Curriculum Vitae · Google Scholar · Email

I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at Esade Business School and a Research Collaborator at the HBS Pricing Lab. My research focuses on macroeconomics, industrial organization, and market power. Prior to joining Esade, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at Harvard Business School. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Zurich in 2023.
